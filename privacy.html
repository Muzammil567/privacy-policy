1. Introduction

StrataMap is an offline 2D infinite concept mapping, node-graph canvas, and architectural diagramming application developed by Sun Saviour. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app. The application operates on a strict privacy-first, offline architecture: it does not collect, record, track, transmit, or monetize any personal data, project diagrams, or concept maps.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

StrataMap does not collect or store any personal or sensitive information on external servers. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (such as name, email address, phone number, or physical address)
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Device identifiers (such as IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID, or MAC address)
  • IP addresses or network connectivity telemetry
  • Usage analytics, behavioral tracking, or crash telemetry
  • Financial, payment, or banking information
  • Diagram titles, node labels, text notes, connection graphs, or project blueprints created in the app
  • Photos, media, calendar events, or contacts outside the app's local sandbox

The application requires no account registration, login credentials, or cloud synchronization.

3. Local Data Storage & User Projects

All concept maps, node structures, connector layouts, board catalogs, and visual parameters created in StrataMap are stored locally on your device only.

  • Data resides strictly in the application's isolated private sandbox using standard Android local storage mechanisms.
  • Data is never transmitted to Sun Saviour, third-party databases, or cloud servers.
  • You maintain complete ownership and control over all diagrams and concept boards.

4. Ephemeral In-Memory Processing & Diagram Export

Real-time canvas gestures, multi-touch pinch-to-zoom scaling, viewport transformations, spring physics simulations, and offscreen bitmap image rendering for user-initiated exports are computed ephemerally in your device's memory (RAM). Exported diagram files are generated only upon your explicit command and saved locally via Android's secure FileProvider mechanism for direct user sharing.

5. Android Permissions

StrataMap operates with zero sensitive Android permissions. The application does not request:

  • Internet access (android.permission.INTERNET is not requested)
  • Location access
  • Camera or Microphone access
  • Contacts or Calendar access
  • External storage read/write access

All infinite canvas modeling, node linking, and diagram export features operate completely offline without network connectivity.

6. Third-Party Services & SDKs

StrataMap maintains a clean, private environment. We do not integrate:

  • Advertising networks or ad SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or AppLovin)
  • Analytics or crash reporting SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Flurry, or Sentry)
  • Social media tracking or login libraries
  • Third-party data brokers or cloud storage backends

7. Children's Privacy

StrataMap is suitable for general audiences and safe for users of all ages, including students and educators. In compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction).
  • The application contains no advertisements, behavioral tracking, or external links leading outside the app environment.

8. Global User Rights (GDPR, CCPA / CPRA & CalOPPA)

Under international privacy regulations including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EU), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA - USA), and CalOPPA:

  • Right to Know / Access: We collect zero personal data; no user or project profiles exist on our end.
  • Right to Deletion: All user content is stored locally on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all boards.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Because no personal data is collected, sold, or shared, opt-out mechanisms are not applicable.

9. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

Your concept maps, infinite boards, and diagram catalogs remain on your device for as long as you keep StrataMap installed. You can permanently erase all stored data at any time by:

  • Clearing the app's cache and data via Android Settings > Apps > StrataMap > Storage > Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the application from your device.

10. Security Standards

Because StrataMap operates completely offline without network connections, your intellectual property, blueprints, and diagrams are protected against remote data breaches, interception, and server compromises. Local data is protected by the built-in application sandboxing and encryption mechanisms of the Android Operating System.

11. International Transfers

No user information is transmitted across international borders because StrataMap has no server infrastructure and does not transfer data over the internet.
